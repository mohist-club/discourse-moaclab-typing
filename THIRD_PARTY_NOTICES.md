# Third-party data

## Complete HSK Vocabulary

- Source: https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary
- License: MIT
- Copyright (c) 2026 Yanis Zafirópulos (aka Dr.Kameleon)

The generated `moaclab-typing-words.js` file contains a filtered subset of the
source vocabulary: 3,000 frequency-ranked Simplified Chinese entries containing
2–4 Han characters, their tone-free pinyin, and their minimum HSK level.

The MIT license text is available at:
https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary/blob/main/LICENSE
